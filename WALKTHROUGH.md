Gone Girl - Walkthrough

This guide provides the step-by-step commands to win the game and see the full story.

*** IMPORTANT: THE NOISE MECHANIC ***
This game has a "noise" mechanic. Actions marked with (NOISY!) will put you in danger.
You MUST type 'hide' as your very next command, or you will lose the game.

---
GAME START (Living Room)
---

1.  pour vase
    (Finds the report, remote, and wood amulet. This is a NOISY! action.)
2.  hide
    (You are now safe.)
3.  get wood_amulet
4.  get report
5.  get remote
6.  get metal_amulet
7.  turnon remote
    (Lights the living room and gives a hint about the main bedroom door.)
8.  read report
    (Gives you the two final questions and a hint.)
9.  go south
    (To Bathroom)
10. open bin
11. get water_amulet
12. get mirror
    (You must get the mirror before you can use it.)
13. use mirror
    (Starts the 3-turn challenge and unlocks Bedroom 2!)

---
THE 3-TURN CHALLENGE
You must get to Bedroom 2 (north of the Kitchen) in 3 turns.
---

14. go north
    (Turn 1: Back to Living Room)
15. go west
    (Turn 2: To Kitchen)
16. go north
    (Turn 3: You are safely in Bedroom 2. The challenge ends. You automatically get the phone and ticket.)

---
COLLECTING THE FINAL PIECES
---

17. get hammer
18. get lamp
19. examine phone
20. examine ticket
21. go south
    (To Kitchen)
22. open pot
23. get fire_amulet
24. go east
    (To Living Room)
25. break door
    (Uses the hammer to open the Main Bedroom door. This is a NOISY! action.)
26. hide
    (You are safe.)
27. go north
    (To Main Bedroom)
28. lift bed
    (Finds the final amulet. This is a NOISY! action.)
29. hide
    (You are safe.)
30. get earth_amulet
    (You now have all 5 "memento" amulets.)

---
THE ENDGAME
---

31. open closet
    (With all 5 amulets, this opens the secret door to the east.)
32. go east
    (You are now in the dark Secret Room.)
33. use lamp
    (This lights the room, revealing the 'bone' and 'wall'.)
34. examine wall
35. examine bone (not necessary)
36. answer here || answer secret room
    (Answers the first question: "Where is the girl?")
37. answer hunger || answer thrist || answer loneliness
    (Answers the second question: "What did this to her?")
    (This final command triggers the game-ending check. You have won.)
